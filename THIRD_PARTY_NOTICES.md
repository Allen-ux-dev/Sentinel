## Inspiration: Sentry

Sentinel's original product inspiration came from [Lakr233/Sentry](https://github.com/Lakr233/Sentry), a macOS security-monitoring application designed to watch device-state changes while the owner steps away.

Sentinel is a separate implementation with its own interface, vector "living eye" interaction system, local event timeline, authentication-failure reaction engine, watchdog architecture, bilingual UI, and optional still-image capture workflow.

Sentry is also released under the MIT License. This attribution is provided to make the project's inspiration and relationship clear.
